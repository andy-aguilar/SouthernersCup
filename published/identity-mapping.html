Why this matters

The league played on ESPN from 2010 to 2021 and on Sleeper from 2022. Nobody carries the same id across both. Until each person is matched, they appear as two different managers and every career record is wrong. A wrong merge is worse than no merge, so anything uncertain is left for you to confirm rather than guessed.

How matches are scored

Two signals: similarity between real names (ESPN stores them; Sleeper usually does not) and similarity between team names across eras — several teams kept their name through the move, which is strong evidence. A surname appearing inside a Sleeper handle also counts. Each ESPN owner is matched to at most one Sleeper manager, best evidence first.
