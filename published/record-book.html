How to read this

All-play is what your record would be if you played every team every week. It measures scoring, not scheduling. Luck is the gap between the record you got and the one your scoring deserved — positive means the schedule was kind to you.

Methodology

Every figure is computed from raw Sleeper league data by history.py, not estimated. All-play compares each team's weekly score against all eleven others. Luck is actual wins minus all-play-expected wins. Careers are merged across Sleeper account changes using the league identity file, so a manager who made a new account still has one history.
